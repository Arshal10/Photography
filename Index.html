Statement

Est. 2016

We still count frames, not files.

Every print on our walls started as a strip of exposed film — no crop tool, no undo. Our founder, a former photojournalist, built the darkroom by hand in 2016 after twenty years shooting on assignment with nothing heavier than a Leica and a light meter.

We develop, contact-print, and hand-select every image before it's ever enlarged. What you see in the gallery is the frame the photographer chose the moment they pressed the shutter — nothing added after.
